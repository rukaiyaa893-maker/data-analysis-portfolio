# Ghana Tourism Patterns Analysis (2017–2025)

## Project Overview
An end-to-end data analytics capstone examining international arrival trends, domestic tourism flows, and post-pandemic sector recovery in Ghana between 2017 and 2025. Using datasets sourced from the Ghana Tourism Authority and international arrival records, this project transforms raw tourism statistics into an interactive business intelligence solution to support data-driven destination management and policy planning.

---

## Tools & Technologies
* **Microsoft Excel:** Initial data inspection, formatting, and dataset structuring (`ghana_tourism_clean1.xlsx`)
* **Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn):** Data cleaning, missing value handling, outlier detection, and exploratory data analysis (EDA)
* **SQL (MySQL / PostgreSQL):** Relational database schema design and analytical querying for multi-year arrival trends
* **Microsoft Power BI:** Star-schema data modeling, DAX measures, and interactive executive dashboard design (`Final Capstone project.pbix`)

---

## Key Insights & Findings
* **Resident-First Growth:** Residents grew by **3.83x** between 2017 and 2025, outstripping non-resident growth (**2.63x**)—highlighting a massive untapped domestic market since 0 of the 12 government interventions specifically targeted residents.
* **Pandemic & Recovery:** Mapped the sharp 2020 contraction (**-68.6%** arrivals) and the multi-year rebound trajectory through 2025.
* **Tourism Concentration:** Found that **76.8%** of domestic site visits concentrate at just 10 major tourist sites, led by Kwame Nkrumah Memorial Park and Kakum National Park.
